How it works

  1. Get your details ready. The checklist below tells you what you need.
  2. Fill in the form once for yourself. Each player of the board fills in his own form.
  3. Press Submit. You will see a thank you page when your form has been received.

Keep these ready

  • Your board.
  • Your display name, the cricketer's name that is verified in the Hand Cricket app, or the one you will have verified.
  • Your real name, written correctly.
  • The email address on which you have Hand Cricket premium.
  • Your WhatsApp number with the country code.
  • Your city and country.

Your agreement (all three required)

To keep this registration fair, IHCC records the following when you submit the form:

  1. Your IP address.
  2. The approximate city, region, country and internet provider that your IP address shows.
  3. Your device type: mobile, tablet or computer, and your browser.
  4. Your browser language and time zone.
  5. The date and time of your submission, and how long you took to fill the form.
  6. The consent you give on this page.

This is used only to find fake or duplicate entries, and to prove that every entry and vote is fair. It is seen only by IHCC management and is never shown to other players. The name, email, WhatsApp number, city and country that you type are used only for IHCC squad registration and communication.

Questions

How many players must a board register?

A board needs at least 12 players. Every player fills in this form himself. There is no upper limit on the form.

Which players can vote?

The first 12 players of a board's squad are its voting members. The board decides the order of its squad.

What if I make a mistake?

The same email address or display name cannot be submitted twice. Tell IHCC in the IHCC group and your entry will be corrected.

Why is my IP address recorded?

Only to keep the registration fair: to find fake or duplicate entries, and later to keep elections fair. It is recorded only if you tick the data and privacy box.

Who can see my details?

Only IHCC management. The page shows other players only a number: how many players each board has registered so far. No names or details are shown.
